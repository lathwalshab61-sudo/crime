## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tech, authoritative, and uncompromising digital workstation for forensic investigators, laboratory analysts, and criminologists. Drawing inspiration from precision tactical displays, advanced laboratory diagnostics, and modern cyber-investigation consoles, the interface prioritizes evidentiary integrity, situational clarity, and rapid intelligence processing.

### Visual Style & Aesthetic Pillars
- **Technical Skeuomorphism & Tactical HUD:** Modern, flat foundational structures layered with surgical micro-grids, coordinate trackers, evidentiary crosshairs, and monospaced telemetry readouts.
- **Deep Contrast & Low Light Ergonomics:** Obsidian deep-space foundations isolate vibrant neon markers, guaranteeing zero visual drift or eye fatigue during continuous investigation workflows.
- **Authoritative Hierarchy:** Crisp geometric boundaries and micro-scale telemetry anchors replace gratuitous decoration. Every pixel must look computed, validated, and logged under chain of custody.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Cyan and amber glows are deployed strictly as functional indicators for status telemetry, bio-metric matches, critical chain-of-custody updates, and focal nodes.

## Colors

The system uses an atmospheric, tactical dark palette calibrated for high-density forensic analysis, evidentiary markup, and data telemetry.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#00E5FF` – Electric Forensic Cyan):** Applied to active telemetry nodes, matched ballistic points, interactive primary targets, calibrated sliders, and focal scan vectors.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` – Deep Sector Blue):** Structural primary fills, active tactical state layers, standard active selection nodes, and auxiliary metric containers.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` – Evidence Amber):** Reserved strictly for physical evidence numbering, hazard tags, chain-of-custody alerts, tamper indicators, and pending ballistic verifications.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#070B14` – Deep Obsidian Void):** Base application backdrop, creating infinite depth behind layered instrumentation surfaces.
- **Surface Foundations:**
  - `surface-base`: `#070B14` (Deep canvas, background floor)
  - `surface-elevated`: `#0D1322` (Secondary panels, sidebar navigations, utility trays)
  - `surface-card`: `#131C31` (Primary analysis modules, incident dossiers, media wrappers)
  - `surface-overlay`: `#1B2642` (Modal dialogs, popovers, contextual inspection layers)
- **Borders & Reticles:**
  - `border-subtle`: `#1E2C4A` (Structural grid dividers, low-priority metric separators)
  - `border-strong`: `#2A3E66` (Card boundaries, active focus rings, tactical container brackets)
  - `border-glow`: `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.4)` (Active hover vectors and high-alert evidence perimeter demarcations)
- **Text & Foreground:**
  - `text-primary`: `#F8FAFC` (Sterile white for high-priority headlines and essential forensic metrics)
  - `text-secondary`: `#94A3B8` (System metadata, auxiliary coordinates, time stamps)
  - `text-accent`: `#38BDF8` (Interactive hover links, sub-telemetry callouts)
  - `text-warning`: `#F59E0B` (Evidence index tags, biological hazard notations)
  - `text-danger`: `#EF4444` (Chain of custody breach, contaminated specimen warnings)

## Typography

Typography establishes an analytical tripartite relationship between structural weight, diagnostic legibility, and tabular data density.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Roles
- **Headings (`Space Grotesk`):** Engineered geometric grotesque with sharp architectural cuts. Used for case titles, section headers, specimen summaries, and diagnostic modal titling.
- **Narrative & Body (`Inter`):** Unobtrusive, neutral screen-optimized body type. Applied to witness statements, forensic pathologist notes, incident summaries, and analytical descriptions.
- **Evidence Telemetry & Metadata (`JetBrains Mono`):** Rigorous, high-legibility monospace typeface. Mandatory for all coordinate positions, DNA sequence readouts, evidence tag serials, timestamps, timestamps with millisecond offsets, and file checksums.
- **Letter Spacing:** Monospace tokens use loose letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.06em`) to preserve character boundaries in low-illumination monitoring environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by a dense, tactical multi-column system designed for parallel telemetry and high data throughput.

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1440px and higher. Uses 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters with 32px (`2rem`) margins. Allows 3-to-4 column modular forensic layouts (e.g., Incident Navigation, Core Dossier / 3D Scene View, Specimen Analysis, Chain of Custody Ledger).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** 768px to 1439px. Uses 16px (`1rem`) gutters with 24px (`1.5rem`) margins. Secondary panels collapse into sliding lateral HUD drawers.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Under 768px. Uses 16px (`1rem`) gutters and margins. Collapses multi-pane monitors into segmented viewports with persistent bottom-pinned telemetry tools.

### Tactical Geometry & Alignment
- All visual spacing adheres strictly to a baseline 4px / 8px incremental scale.
- Components use consistent inset padding: inputs and badges rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while dense evidence dossiers leverage `space-lg` to separate scientific evidence cards from surrounding micro-grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through tonal layering, crisp multi-stage borders, and neon photonic luminescence rather than soft diffuse shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation - `#070B14`):** Canvas backdrop, rendered with an optional 32px CSS micro-dot grid pattern (`rgba(42, 62, 102, 0.2)`).
- **Tier 1 (Containment - `#0D1322`):** Primary workspaces, peripheral tool rails, and spatial map wrappers. Border: 1px solid `#1E2C4A`.
- **Tier 2 (Evidence Modules - `#131C31`):** Interactive data cards, 3D point cloud inspect modules, case documents. Border: 1px solid `#2A3E66`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating HUD / Overlays - `#1B2642`):** Inspection modals, quick-action menus, bio-metric overlays. Border: 1px solid `#38BDF8`.

### Tactical Illumination & Shadows
- **Quiescent Shadow:** `0 4px 20px rgba(3, 7, 18, 0.75)` for elevated containers.
- **Active Cyan Radiation:** `0 0 16px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.25), 0 0 2px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.8)` applied to primary triggers, targeted 3D points, and selected evidence nodes.
- **Evidence Warning Radiation:** `0 0 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25), 0 0 2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.8)` applied to flagged contraband, critical match badges, and biological hazards.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a precise, low-radius shape profile (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base border radius) evoking military hardware, laboratory sensors, and scientific monitors.

### Architectural Corner Rules
- **Standard Controls & Badges (`rounded-sm`, 4px):** Applied to buttons, data tags, inputs, and chips. Communicates rigidity, durability, and computational exactness.
- **Structural Cards & Viewports (`rounded-lg`, 8px):** Outer wrappers, forensic image containers, and floating panels.
- **Chamfered / Notched Accents:** Select high-priority modules utilize clipped-corner styling (45-degree corner clips at 6px) to emulate rugged industrial monitors and evidentiary packaging labels.
- **Status Indicators (`pill-shaped`, 9999px):** Retained solely for miniature telemetry status bulbs and dynamic counter indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Tactical Action:** Background `#00E5FF`, label `#070B14` (Space Grotesk, Bold, Uppercase), corner radius 4px. Resting box-shadow `0 0 12px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.3)`. Hover: `#38BDF8` with expanded cyan halo.
- **Secondary Inspection Action:** Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`, border 1px solid `#2563EB`, text `#38BDF8`. Hover: background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.3)`.
- **Destructive / Flag Action:** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`, border 1px solid `#EF4444`, text `#FCA5A5`.

### Evidence Badges & Chips
- **Evidence Tag Marker:** Background `#131C31`, border 1px solid `#F59E0B`, text `#F59E0B` (JetBrains Mono). Features a left-hand amber badge marker displaying the item code (e.g., `TAG-0824`) alongside an amber status pip.
- **Status Pills:** Background `#0D1322`, border 1px solid `#1E2C4A`, text `#94A3B8`. Displays a 6px pulsing dot (Cyan = Active Telemetry, Amber = Chain Check, Green = Confirmed Match, Red = Contaminated).

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Base fill `#131C31` with 1px border `#2A3E66`.
- **Forensic Card Header:** Equipped with coordinate tick-marks or hairline brackets in the corners (`+` crosshairs in top-right corner). Monospaced title with sterile white font and sub-metadata index string.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#0D1322`, border 1px solid `#1E2C4A`, text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Font: JetBrains Mono for alphanumeric search (Case IDs, Lat/Long, DNA sequences). Focused state activates border `#00E5FF` with a subtle cyan inset glow.
- **Selection Dropdowns:** Deep obsidian drawer `#0D1322` with hairline dividers `#1E2C4A` and active item highlighted in electric cyan text with faint blue backdrop.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** 16x16px square, radius 2px, border 1px solid `#2A3E66`, background `#0D1322`. Checked: background `#00E5FF`, border `#00E5FF`, with `#070B14` tactical check mark.
- **Radio Button:** 16x16px circle, border 1px solid `#2A3E66`, background `#0D1322`. Active: concentric inner ring illuminated with `#00E5FF`.

### Forensic Domain Components
- **Chain of Custody Ledger Row:** Alternating low-contrast background rows (`#0D1322` and `#111827`) separated by `#1E2C4A`, with monospaced cryptographic hash tags, badge-stamped timestamps, and digital officer signatures.
- **Ballistics / Spectral Match Bar:** Horizontal calibrated progress scale with segmented cyan/amber ticks indicating probability coefficients (e.g., `99.4% IDENT`).
- **Tactical Crosshair Reticle:** Visual targeting component placed over evidence imagery, featuring a 24x24px cyan target bracket, precise X/Y pixel telemetry readout, and dynamic magnification factor label.
